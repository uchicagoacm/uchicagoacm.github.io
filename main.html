About Us

Mission Statement

UChicago ACM strives to foster communication and collaboration within the University's computer science community and other technical communities, supporting members' professional growth through opportunities for lifelong learning, career development, and professional networking.

Why You Should Join

UChicago ACM is the University of Chicago's student chapter of the world's largest educational and scientific computing society, the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).

As a student chapter, our mission is to promote the ACM's goal of "advancing computing as a science and a profession" amongst the University of Chicago's students. We currently have two committees in machine learning and software engineering that meet weekly to give students hands-on experience outside of the classroom.

UChicago ACM also holds general meetings that give students the opportunity to connect and learn from professionals in various industries of computer science.

Machine Learning Committee

About

Senior ML/AI Director: Konnor Lo
Junior ML/AI Director: Jason Zhang

The ML Committee is devoted to providing students with any computer science experience the opportunity to develop machine learning knowledge. The committee starts with intensive, interactive ramp-up workshops throughout the winter quarter, followed by an onboarding project activity.

  • In-person and virtual workshops ranging from basic to advanced ML/AI topics
  • Hands-on coding sessions to implement a diverse variety of machine learning models
  • A skilled community open to collaborating on global ML/AI challenges
  • Guest lectures from industry experts and field-defining researchers

Software Engineering Committee

About

Senior SWE Director: Anwar Kader
Junior SWE Director: Xander Hung

The SWE Committee prioritizes cultivating essential technical and practical skills for professional development. Activities include interview preparation, lessons on version control, exposure to agile development, and hands-on project work.

  • Interview prep with focus on algorithms and data structures
  • GitHub, version control, and collaborative workflows
  • Agile development and product management
  • Hands-on side projects and quarterly group projects
