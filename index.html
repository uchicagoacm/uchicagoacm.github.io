01

Advancing computing as a science and a profession

UChicago ACM fosters communication and collaboration within the University's computer science community — supporting members' growth through lifelong learning, career development, and professional networking.

As a student chapter of the world's largest educational and scientific computing society, we run two hands-on committees — machine learning and software engineering — that meet weekly to take students beyond the classroom. Our general meetings connect students with professionals across every corner of the industry.

  • Hands-on coding sessions across model families
